## Challenge

--- task ---
Add a buzzer that sounds when the LED turns on — use another pin and `Buzzer()` from `picozero`.
--- /task ---

--- task ---
Show the water level using two LEDs (green for when there is enough water, and red for when the tank is too empty).
--- /task ---

--- task ---
Add a small display (like an OLED display) to show the exact distance, or a 'percentage full'.
--- /task ---

--- task ---
If the LED flickers when the tank is nearly at the 'too empty' point, add a small delay or buffer value to make it smoother.
--- /task ---

--- task ---
**Upgrade:** Use the [Touch-free tap](https://projects.raspberrypi.org/en/projects/touch-free-tap){:target="_blank"} project to help you automatically refill the tank from another water source when the water level is too low. You will need to use a relay, a power source, and a submersible pump.
--- /task ---
