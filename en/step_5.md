## Add the LED Indicator

--- task ---
Insert the LED into the breadboard.  
Place the legs in separate rows so you can connect a resistor and wires easily.
--- /task ---

--- task ---
Connect:
| LED leg | Connect to |
|----------|-------------|
| Long (anode) | GP15 through 220 Ω resistor |
| Short (cathode) | GND |
--- /task ---

--- task ---
Check that the LED lights when you briefly connect its long leg to 3.3 V (to confirm orientation).
--- /task ---
